Privacy Policy

Application: Gong Shuttle (Wollongong Free Bus) • Effective Date: November 7, 2026
Key Summary: We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. Location access is used strictly on-device in real time to locate your nearest bus stops and estimate walking distances.

1. Overview and Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how the Gong Shuttle mobile application handles your information. The application was built with a strict privacy-by-design approach: we do not collect personal identifiers, we do not require account registration, and we do not bundle tracking or profiling analytics SDKs.

2. Location Data and Permissions

The app requests access to device location services (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION).

  • Why Location is Needed: The Wollongong Free Shuttle operates as two continuous loops (Route 55C Clockwise and Route 55A Anti-Clockwise). Along major roads like Crown St, Burelli St, and Northfields Ave, stops for both directions are located within 15–40 meters of each other across the street. Precise location is required to identify which side of the street you are standing on and calculate accurate walking distance.
  • On-Device Processing: Your GPS coordinates are processed entirely in memory on your phone using the mathematical Haversine formula against a local catalog of bus stops.
  • Zero Server Storage: Your location is never transmitted to our servers, never stored in any database, and never shared with third parties.
  • No Background Location: The app does not request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. It does not track your location when the app is closed.

3. Transit Data & Network Communication

To display real-time departures, the app queries public transit endpoints via a secure Cloudflare Worker reverse proxy connected to the Transport for NSW (TfNSW) Open Data Hub.

  • Only public transit parameters (such as bus stop IDs and general Wollongong coordinates) are sent.
  • No personal identification data, Advertising IDs (AAID), or device fingerprints are transmitted.
  • All network communications are encrypted with standard HTTPS (TLS).

4. Non-Affiliation Government Disclaimer

Gong Shuttle is an independent transit tool and is NOT an official government application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Transport for NSW or the NSW Government. Transit schedules and real-time vehicle locations are sourced from the public Transport for NSW Open Data Hub under Creative Commons licensing.

5. Third-Party Links & Sponsors

  • Google Maps Navigation: Tapping "Directions" opens your device's native Google Maps app to provide walking directions to the selected bus stop.
  • Sponsor Banner: The app includes an informational link to TaskPasser (taskpasser.com) in the bottom bar. Clicking this banner opens your default web browser. No tracking pixels or cross-app ad network SDKs are embedded in the app.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because no personal data is collected or sent to remote servers, there is no personal data to delete. You can clear local preferences at any time through your Android App Settings (Settings > Apps > Gong Shuttle > Storage > Clear Data).

7. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age, complying with COPPA and the Australian Privacy Principles.
